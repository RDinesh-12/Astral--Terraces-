Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026 | Effective Date: November 25, 2026
BuildInTamil Studio ("we", "us", or "our") operates the video game Astral Terraces and related services. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and shared when you play our games or interact with our services across web browsers, YouTube Playables, and Facebook Instant Games.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization and user privacy. Astral Terraces does not require personal account creation or collect personal identification documents.

  • Gameplay and Progress Data: We store level progression, collected star relics, high scores, elapsed times, and user preferences (audio volume, controls, visual quality) locally on your device using browser LocalStorage.
  • Platform Account Data: When played through third-party platforms such as Facebook Instant Games or YouTube Playables, the platform SDK provides non-sensitive player identifier tokens or public display names solely to display in-game leaderboards or save game state to the platform's cloud storage.
  • Technical Information: Standard non-identifiable technical metrics (screen resolution, browser type, device orientation) processed strictly on your client device to adapt camera viewports and touch controls.

2. How We Use Information

Any data processed by Astral Terraces is used exclusively to:

  • Provide, operate, and save your game progression across puzzle worlds.
  • Synchronize audio mute states with the host platform (e.g. YouTube player or Facebook Messenger).
  • Submit high scores to session leaderboards with your consent.

3. Third-Party Platform Services

Our game may be distributed through third-party platforms that maintain their own privacy policies:

  • Meta / Facebook Gaming: Operates under Meta's Privacy Policy. Instant Games SDK interacts with Facebook cloud storage to persist your game score.
  • YouTube Playables / Google: Operates under Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your local game progress is stored safely in your browser's private storage area. You can reset or delete this data at any time directly through the in-game Settings menu ("New Game / Reset Progress") or by clearing your browser's local cache.

5. Children's Privacy

Our games are designed for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or store personal information from children under the age of 13.
